# Sponsors

This is the canonical sponsor page for [@psyb0t](https://github.com/psyb0t) — the open-source work, the tools, the experiments, the chaos.

If you've ever shipped something on top of one of my projects, saved a weekend because a tool I built already existed, or just liked the vibe — this is where you can throw some fuel on the fire.

**→ [Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/psyb0t)**

## What sponsorship funds

- Time to keep maintaining the projects that already exist (issues, PRs, releases)
- Time to build the next ones instead of taking on more client work
- Infra costs (CI, registries, hosting for demos)
- Instant noodles. A lot of instant noodles.

## Monthly tiers

### $3 / month

Tip jar. You get a sponsor badge on your GitHub profile and the warm feeling of supporting random open source. No listing, no obligations — just vibes.

### $10 / month

Your name or logo listed on this page, with a link to wherever you want (your site, your GitHub, your company).

### $25 / month

Everything above, **plus** your name/logo in the README of any of my public projects you want to be associated with. Pick one, pick five, pick whatever fits. Good for companies that want visible association with a specific tool (e.g. a security firm next to a security tool).

## One-time tiers

### $5 one-time

Tip jar. Sponsor badge + the warm feeling of supporting random open source. Thanks for the instant noodles.

### $50 one-time

Above + your name or logo on this page **for a year**, with a link to wherever you want (your site, your GitHub, your company).

### Custom

DM me. Happy to talk priority feature work, private support, or one-off thank-yous.
